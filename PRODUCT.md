# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/vanilla JavaScript; selected to match the user's explicit requirement and keep the GitHub Pages publishing workflow simple.

## Users

- Shoppers browsing jewellery, comparing items, adding products to a cart and reviewing their demo orders.
- Store owner/manager maintaining a product catalogue, stock counts and order statuses.

## Product Purpose

A luxury jewellery storefront prototype that can be published as a static GitHub Pages demo and used to validate catalogue and store-management interactions before a secure commerce backend is connected.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product categories, search, category/price/availability filters, sorting, cart, demo checkout, browser-local accounts/order history, and client-side product/inventory/order management.
- GitHub Pages is static hosting; current account, order and inventory behavior is browser-local and is not production authentication, shared inventory, database persistence or online payment.
- Brand name, actual inventory, prices, warranty details, store contact, policies, legal details, and payment provider remain placeholders/open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

The user requested a luxury gold/jewellery store with a palette of brown, gold and royal green. Persian RTL interface. Keep paths standard for GitHub Pages.

## Evidence on Hand

No verified product data, brand assets, authenticity certificates, store contact details, policies or payment integration were supplied. Product names, prices, inventory, and imagery are clearly sample content.

## Product Principles

- Make product discovery and filtering straightforward.
- Treat stock and order states as explicit information.
- Keep the luxury presentation refined without obscuring price or availability.
- Label demo-only behavior honestly; never imply payment or server-side security exists.
